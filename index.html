Active Aging Bingo

A quick wellness break while we learn together.

How to Play

1
Choose Your Card Select the card number assigned to you.
2
Listen for an Activity We will call activities throughout the presentation.
3
Complete the Activity Do the activity or think of your answer, then tap the matching square.
4
Get 3 Lines Complete any 3 full lines across, down, or diagonally.
Got 3 full lines? Yell BINGO! Please interrupt us — we want to hear it!

Wellness Is Personal

There is more than one way to participate.

Move Your Way Keep movements comfortable and complete them from your chair.
Think Your Way Your answers are yours — there is no need to share them.
Make It Work for You Modify or skip anything that does not feel right for your body.
