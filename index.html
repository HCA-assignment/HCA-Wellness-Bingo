Active Aging Bingo

A little wellness, one square at a time.

How to Play

Select your assigned card number below. During the presentation, tap or click a square when that activity is called. Tap it again if you marked it by mistake. The centre WELLNESS BREAK space is free. If you complete a line during the presentation, yell BINGO! Please interrupt us — we want to hear it!
B I N G O
Wellness can look different for everyone. Activities can be modified, completed seated, simplified, or adapted to match a person's interests, comfort and abilities.
